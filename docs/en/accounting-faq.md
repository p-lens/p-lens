# Appendix A — Official Interpretations

These interpretations set out how the P-Lens Accounting Standards (PLAS), Edition 1.0.1 (enacted 13 October 2026, amended 20 October 2026; hereinafter "the Standards"), are to be read. This text is a translation; the Japanese text is the original, and where the two differ the Japanese holds.

### Q1. Why are special prizes not entered as assets?

A. Because in this system the booking ends with the prize exchange (Article 8, paragraph 1 of the Standards). The equity accounts are used as a terminal treatment particular to this system (Article 9, paragraph 2 of the Standards).

### Q2. But special prizes have economic value, don't they?

A. They do. That economic value exists and that the transactions afterwards are booked are separate matters.

### Q3. What happens when special prizes are turned into cash?

A. It is outside what this system books as standard (Article 7, paragraph 4 of the Standards).

### Q4. Why is it outside?

A. Because it was laid down to be outside.

### Q5. Is that proper, as accounting?

A. It differs from the classification of assets in general financial accounting. It is a treatment resting on a purpose of management accounting particular to this system (Supplementary Provisions, paragraph 2 of the Standards).

### Q6. Cash put in is recorded, and cash transactions after the prize exchange are not?

A. Your understanding is correct.

### Q7. Is that not a contradiction?

A. It is not. Limiting the scope of what is booked and the accuracy of the booking are separate matters.

### Q8. Is Yakult received as a prize equity too?

A. In general accounting theory, not necessarily. In this system's terminal accounts it is treated as equity (Article 10, paragraph 2 of the Standards).

### Q9. Does equity not fall when the Yakult is drunk?

A. This system does not follow the consumption of prizes (Article 12, paragraph 1 of the Standards).

### Q10. So in the book it has not been drunk?

A. No. The book simply says nothing on whether it was drunk.

### Q11. Then does it not fail to reflect what really happened?

A. This system does not record everything that really happens. It records what has been defined as what it records.

### Q12. I do want to record transactions after the prize exchange.

A. This system is open-source software. It may be modified freely within the terms of the licence that applies.

### Q13. So officially it will not be supported?

A. Your understanding is correct.

### Q14. Are there plans to support it in future?

A. There are not.

### Q15. Is it necessary to be so categorical?

A. It is, so that no ambiguity is left in the interpretation of the Standards.
